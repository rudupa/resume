01 About Me

I’m an embedded software architect with 13+ years of experience building system software for radar/ADAS, powertrain, and high-performance automotive compute platforms. My background spans embedded C/C++, Linux, AUTOSAR, multicore SoCs, and safety-critical development.

I care about architecting platforms that scale — middleware and interfaces that hold up across product generations — and about the people side of that: leading cross-functional teams, and working directly with OEMs and silicon partners on next-generation vehicle compute and sensor systems.

More recently I’ve been pulled toward the edge of that world — running local inference on platforms like the NVIDIA Jetson AGX Thor, and building agentic engineering tools (I call it “vibe coding”) that power CI/CT automation and chat-driven project assistants. I’m also exploring what AI-native IDEs look like day to day, and on the automotive side, working on VLA and VLM models for autonomous driving and how to wrap them in safety layers that keep deterministic systems predictable even as the cognitive layer around them gets less so.
